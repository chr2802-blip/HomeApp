## The idea

The middle tile at the top of `/baby` said "Mad" and counted from when the last feed
*started*. Asked: say "Amning" when the last feed was a breast feed, and count from when
it ended.

## The route

`lastFeedAt` replaced by `lastFeed` (method, running, the instant to count from) in
`src/lib/baby.ts`; the tile reads it. Mid-push the user asked for more (a tile pressed
shows only that kind, with the time between entries — "vågen" for sleep, from another
app's screenshot), so the push was stopped, the tiles became buttons narrowing the list
(`?show=`, kept by the day pager), screenshots sent, and everything pushed together.

## Decided rather than known

- "Which feed is last" is the one with the latest counting instant (a breast feed's end,
  else its start); a running breast feed always wins, and the tile then reads "Nu",
  highlighted, "siden" its start.
- Bottle and solids both say "Mad".
- The overview is the same `/baby` page narrowed, not a page of its own: the day pager,
  strip and dock stay, and "Vis alt" or the tile again undoes it. A gap under a minute is
  not written.
